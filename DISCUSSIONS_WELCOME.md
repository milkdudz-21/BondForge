# Welcome to BondForge Discussions! ⚒️

This is the community space for BondForge — Craft • Profit • Progress.

Share your crafting workflows, ideas for future versions, and questions about profit or Bond tracking.

**Start here:**
- **Q&A:** Ask how a feature works or get help with setup.
- **Ideas:** Suggest future improvements and discuss priorities.
- **Show and tell:** Share your progress or HUD layout (hide private information).
- **General:** Talk about BondForge and crafting strategies.

For reproducible bugs, please use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/milkdudz-21/BondForge/issues/new/choose) so they can be tracked and fixed.

**Reminder:** Profit and GP/hour are estimates, not verified Grand Exchange sales. Please be respectful and never share passwords, account details, or private information.
